Aggressive mode{% if aggressive.on %} ON{% if aggressive.since %} since {{ aggressive.since.strftime('%m-%d %H:%M') }} UTC{% endif %}{% else %} off{% endif %}

Pushes risk and leverage in pursuit of the goal of 1% a day. While it is on:

  • More risk and leverage: the risk per trade goes up and the full leverage ceiling is used at once (table below).
  • No locks: no daily loss stop, no direction lock, no drawdown pause, no automatic brake and no daily limit on trades per coin. It keeps trading whatever the day's result.
  • Day goal: as soon as a ledger stands {{ '%.0f'|format(aggressive.take_profit * 100) }}% above where the day started, after every fee and the borrow interest, all its open trades are closed and the profit is banked. The next {{ '%.0f'|format(aggressive.take_profit * 100) }}% then counts from the banked level, so the bot keeps trading: a new decision cycle starts within half a minute, unless the regular one is less than {{ aggressive.goal_gap }} minutes away. A single trade that makes the goal on its own is closed the same way. Prices are checked every ten seconds, so it closes at or a little above the goal.
  • Single trades: once a trade is {{ '%.0f'|format(aggressive.take_trade * 100) }}% up on the margin put in, net of both fees, the take is armed: the trade stays open while its return keeps rising and is closed when it has given back {{ '%.0f'|format(aggressive.giveback * 100) }}% of its best return, never below the {{ '%.0f'|format(aggressive.take_trade * 100) }}%. A run to 4% is banked near 3%, not at 2%. Its target is only reached when the price gets there first.
  • Stops with more room: every stop sits {{ aggressive.stop_room }} times further from the entry than the managers put it. The position is made smaller to match, so the loss at the stop stays the same.
  • Short holds: no trade is held longer than {{ aggressive.hold }} hours.

What still protects the account: each trade's own stop, the liquidation guard, the kill switch, and a brake you set by hand or that a balance mismatch sets. Nothing else. A run of losing trades is not stopped, so the ledger can lose most of its value. It aims for the goal; it does not guarantee it. It applies from the next cycle, and open positions keep their stops and time limits. Once the bot is live, the go-live leverage ramp still caps leverage.
