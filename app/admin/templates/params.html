The one mode v14

One bot, one book, one config (owner 2026-10-26). What used to be the aggressive profile is the bot; there is no switch.

  • The book: {{ aggressive.assets|join(', ') }} in one pool. Each trade puts up {{ '%.0f'|format(aggressive.share * 100) }}% of equity as margin, at most {{ aggressive.lev }}x.
  • The stop cap: the loss at the stop is at most {{ '%.0f'|format(aggressive.stop_cap * 100) }}% of the margin; the leverage comes down until it fits, and a stop that does not fit at 1x blocks the trade. The risk per trade follows: {{ '%.2f'|format(aggressive.risk * 100) }}% of equity at the cap (code never lets it exceed {{ '%.0f'|format(aggressive.risk_cap * 100) }}%). Stops sit {{ aggressive.stop_room }}× where the managers put them.
  • Day goal: as soon as a ledger stands {{ '%.0f'|format(aggressive.take_profit * 100) }}% above its base, after every fee and the borrow interest, all its open trades are banked and a new decision cycle starts, unless the regular one is within {{ aggressive.goal_gap }} minutes.
  • Single trades: once a trade is {{ '%.0f'|format(aggressive.take_trade * 100) }}% up on the margin put in, net of both fees, the take is armed. The floor is that level plus {{ '%.0f'|format(aggressive.keep * 100) }}% of any gain beyond it: a run to 4% banks at least 3%, a dip after a touch of {{ '%.0f'|format(aggressive.take_trade * 100) }}% banks {{ '%.0f'|format(aggressive.take_trade * 100) }}%.
  • Holds: no trade is held longer than {{ aggressive.hold }} hours.
  • No locks: no daily loss stop, no direction lock, no drawdown pause, no daily limit on trades per coin. Exposure is capped at {{ aggressive.beta }}× equity, beta-weighted.

What protects the account: each trade's own stop and hard stop, the liquidation guard, the kill switch, a brake set by hand or by a balance mismatch, and the emergency brake at {{ '%.0f'|format(aggressive.brake * 100) }}% against starting capital. A run of losing trades inside that is not stopped. The values are the [trading] section of config.toml; the ones below can be set here.
