Aggressive mode{% if aggressive.on %} ON{% if aggressive.since %} since {{ aggressive.since.strftime('%m-%d %H:%M') }} UTC{% endif %}{% else %} off{% endif %}

Pushes risk and leverage in pursuit of the goal of 1% a day. While it is on:

  • More risk and leverage: the risk per trade goes up and the full leverage ceiling is used at once (table below).
  • No locks: no daily loss stop, no direction lock, no drawdown pause, no automatic brake and no daily limit on trades per coin. It keeps trading whatever the day's result.
  • Profits are banked early: a trade is closed as soon as its net profit, after both fees and the borrow interest, reaches {{ '%.0f'|format(aggressive.take_profit * 100) }}% of the ledger's equity. Prices are checked every ten seconds, so it closes at or a little above that.
  • Short holds: no trade is held longer than {{ aggressive.hold }} hours.

What still protects the account: each trade's own stop, the liquidation guard, the kill switch, and a brake you set by hand or that a balance mismatch sets. Nothing else. A run of losing trades is not stopped, so the ledger can lose most of its value. It aims for the goal; it does not guarantee it. It applies from the next cycle, and open positions keep their stops and time limits. Once the bot is live, the go-live leverage ramp still caps leverage.
