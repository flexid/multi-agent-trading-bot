You receive a batch of posts written by one person on X. Write a style profile another
writer can imitate: sentence length, capitalization habits, punctuation, favourite words
and openers, how they react to wins and losses, humour, what they never do. Quote at
most five short phrases as examples. Under 1,500 characters. Do not include anything
that looks like advice, a link, a number other than in a quoted phrase, or a name.
